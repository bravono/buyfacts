Chapter 1 · Cross-Functional Priorities

Risk / Control / Reward Exploratory Role Alignment

Unlike developmental TRIADs, a Risk / Control / Reward TRIAD is not an upward ladder: movement toward Risk or Control is not inherently worse than movement toward Reward. It reveals how functional roles diverge during evaluation (Market Pull vs. Market Push).

Marketing / Revenue (Evaluator & Sponsor) Dominant Priority: Reward (Speed & Growth)
IT / Development (IT Operations) Dominant Priority: Risk (Integration & Migration)
Finance (Procurement) Dominant Priority: Control (Recurring Cost Exposure)
Operations (User) Dominant Priority: Control (Implementation Governance)
CMO Decision Takeaway: Averaging across functions can conceal an emerging obstacle. By tracking role-specific corner movements across waves, marketing and product leaders gain time to address integration proof and cost governance before renewal is at risk.
