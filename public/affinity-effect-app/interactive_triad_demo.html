TRIAD puck geometry and scoring demo

The puck begins at the centroid. Use the corner buttons or click a corner location. Each submitted corner records its prescribed sentiment-point value; interior points are not scored.

Unsubmitted starting position: center (no sentiment points)

Geometry: left (140,480), right (560,480), apex (350, 480 − 420 × √3/2). All three sides = 420 SVG units. At a corner, the puck is tangent to both inner edges of the triangle border. The saved response is the exact selected corner key.
