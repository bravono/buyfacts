TRIAD Introduction

Welcome to TRIAD, a method that transforms how we evaluate sentiment, affinity, and value. TRIAD’s framework reveals trends and provides insights to align marketing with customer sentiment.

By focusing on perception and experience, TRIAD helps identify areas driving positive buzz and purchase decisions. Let’s see how TRIAD simplifies decision-making and maximizes campaign impact.

TRIAD Benefit

Measure Movement: Reveals shifts in sentiment and affinity, offering insights into customer behavior.

Enhances Decision-Making: Provides direction on marketing areas delivering the most value and positive sentiment.

Builds Alignment: Aligns perception and experience, helping teams focus on areas that create buzz and purchase intent.

TRIAD Results

Improved Campaign ROI: Better targeted marketing increases impact on awareness, interest, and purchase behavior.

Stronger Affinity Metrics: Identifies areas boosting customer affinity and loyalty, turning buyers into advocates.

Clearer Insight into Sentiment: Delivers a nuanced understanding of what drives customer perceptions and actions.

TRIAD Next Steps

Identify Key Marketing Areas: Focus on campaigns, programs, or assets with the highest potential for sentiment improvement.

Set Sentiment Benchmarks: Establish baseline metrics to track how TRIAD insights influence affinity and purchase behavior.

Implement and Iterate: Use TRIAD insights to refine your marketing strategy and adjust based on emerging trends and customer responses.
